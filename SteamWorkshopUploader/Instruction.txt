The "WorkshopContent" folder contains your addons.

This folder should contain folders with addons, 
which should contain the your addon files (As you can see in the example). 

Click on the "Create item" button to create 
the new item to upload. (The window above is the name for creating the item.)

Updating:
If you want to update an item, then the updated material must have significant changes, 
otherwise the update will not be uploaded to the workshop.
